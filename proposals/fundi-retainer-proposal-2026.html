Terms and Conditions

This proposal sets out the terms governing the monthly retainer engagement between Gro Digital (Pty) Ltd and Fundi Capital (Pty) Ltd.

  1. Invoicing. Gro Digital will issue a tax invoice on or before the last business day of each month. Payment is due within 7 days of the invoice date by EFT. Overdue payments beyond 14 days may result in work being paused until the outstanding balance is settled.
  2. Scope and additional work. Services will be delivered as defined in this proposal. Any requests for work that fall materially outside the defined scope will be discussed with the Client and quoted separately before commencement. Gro Digital will not proceed with out-of-scope work without prior written authorisation.
  3. Initial term and renewal. The initial term of this retainer is 12 months from the commencement date, renewable by mutual written agreement. Either party may request a scope or fee review with 30 days' written notice. Clients who commit to a 2 or 3-year term will have annual fee increases capped at the prevailing South African CPI rate. Clients on annual terms are not subject to this cap — fees at renewal will reflect market rates and scope at that time.
  4. Notice period. Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days' written notice. During the notice period, Gro Digital will continue to deliver services and the Client will continue to pay the monthly fee.
  5. Scaling. The scope and monthly fee may be scaled up or down by mutual written agreement with 30 days' notice. Additional capacity beyond the defined scope is available at Gro Digital's standard day rates.
  6. Third-party relationships. Where Gro Digital manages third-party relationships on behalf of the Client — including the Yenza development partnership for FundiMatch/FundiRecruit — Gro Digital will act as the primary point of coordination. Any third-party costs (including Yenza development fees) are not included in this retainer and will be billed separately at cost.
  7. Intellectual property. All intellectual property developed by Gro Digital for the Client under this retainer transfers to Fundi Capital upon receipt of full payment for the relevant month. Gro Digital retains the right to reference the engagement in general terms in its portfolio and marketing materials, unless the Client requests otherwise in writing.
  8. Confidentiality. Both parties agree to treat as confidential all commercially sensitive information shared during the course of this engagement — including business strategies, pricing, customer data, and technical architecture. This obligation survives the termination of this agreement.
  9. Limitation of liability. Gro Digital's total liability to the Client for any claim arising under or in connection with this agreement is limited to the total value of fees paid by the Client to Gro Digital in the three months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim. Gro Digital is not liable for indirect, consequential, or loss-of-profit damages.
  10. Governing law. This agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of South Africa. Any disputes arising from this agreement will be subject to the jurisdiction of the South African courts.
