We've built four distinct directions for you to click through. Each one has a different energy — not just a different colour. Tell us which feeling resonates and we apply Addex's palette and content to it. You said bold, punchy, in-your-face — we treat your Canva mockup as the floor.

Direction 01 — Raw & Dominant
Our recommendation
bold-brown.vercel.app ↗
Heavy display type, brutalist layout, high contrast — text fills the screen. The "NO EXCUSES / ONLY RESULTS" energy maps directly to "intent finds opportunity." This direction would be genuinely unusual in South African ad-tech. It would stand out. It's the closest to your Canva mockup, but bolder.

Direction 02 — Editorial Dramatic
dramatic-phi.vercel.app ↗
Fashion-editorial layout — large imagery, asymmetric grid, strong vertical rhythm. Feels premium and curated. Works well if you want to lean into the "discover amazing SA businesses" consumer story. Carries some risk of feeling more lifestyle than ad-tech.
Editorial Asymmetric grid Premium feel
Direction 03 — Artistic & Crafted
artistic-puce.vercel.app ↗
Warm, organic aesthetic. Curved type, tactile surfaces, scroll-driven motion. Beautiful for the consumer discovery story. Weaker for the SME advertiser pitch — better suited to Phase 2 when consumer growth becomes the focus.
Organic Consumer-first Motion-rich
Direction 04 — Clean SaaS
flat-woad.vercel.app ↗
Modern flat design, clean grids, standard SaaS layout. Very readable, very safe. Won't put anyone off — won't stop anyone mid-scroll either. Best if the priority is information clarity over brand impact.
Flat SaaS feel Low risk
