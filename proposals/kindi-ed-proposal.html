Project Quotation

Kindi-Ed MVP Development

GroDigital proposes a purpose-built Progressive Web App (PWA) tutoring platform for Kindi-Ed — supporting students, tutors, parents, and administrators, aligned with the South African CAPS curriculum. The platform will enable live tutoring sessions with real-time activity monitoring, structured lesson progression, and a full content management system for your curriculum team. It is designed to validate your business model as an MVP, with a scalable architecture ready to grow internationally beyond launch. Delivery is fixed-fee, targeting Q3/Q4 2026 — well ahead of your June 2027 relocation to South Africa.
