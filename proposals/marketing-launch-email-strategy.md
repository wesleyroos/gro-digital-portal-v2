# GRO Digital — Marketing Services Launch Email Sequence

## Campaign Overview

**Goal:** Announce that GRO Digital now offers marketing as a managed service for clients.
**Audience:** Existing GRO Digital clients + warm subscribers who've engaged with the website/brand.
**Sequence type:** Product update / new service launch — 4 emails over 12 days.
**Tone:** Bold, direct, results-driven. No fluff. Mirrors grodigital.co.za brand voice.
**Primary CTA:** Book a marketing strategy call / see the marketing add-on packages.

---

## 4-Email Sequence

### Email 1 — The Announcement (Day 1)
**Subject:** Your marketing is now handled.
**Preview:** GRO Digital just added something new — and it changes everything about your digital presence.

**Purpose:** Land the announcement. Make it feel like a big deal because it is. One clear message: GRO now does your marketing too.
**CTA:** See what's included → grodigital.co.za
**Key message:** You already trust us with your website. Now let us grow the audience that visits it.

---

### Email 2 — What's Actually Included (Day 4)
**Subject:** Instagram, email campaigns, and more — all handled.
**Preview:** Here's exactly what the GRO marketing service covers — and why it works better when it's connected to your site.

**Purpose:** Remove vagueness. Spell out exactly what they get: social content calendar, Instagram posting, email campaigns (mailers to subscribers), AI-assisted content generation. Connect the "web + marketing = full stack" story.
**CTA:** View marketing packages → grodigital.co.za/packages
**Key message:** Most agencies split web and marketing across different vendors. We connect them — which means better results and less chaos for you.

---

### Email 3 — The "Why This Matters Now" (Day 8)
**Subject:** Having a great website isn't enough anymore.
**Preview:** Your competitors are showing up in feeds, inboxes, and search results every week. Your website alone can't do that.

**Purpose:** Create urgency through insight. Educate without being preachy. Use the real stat that most SA SMBs are losing potential customers to competitors who are more active digitally. Position GRO as the complete answer.
**CTA:** Let's build your marketing plan → [contact/calendar link]
**Key message:** Your website is a destination. Marketing is what makes people show up. You need both, and they need to work together.

---

### Email 4 — Launch Offer / Last Push (Day 12)
**Subject:** First month on us — but only for a few clients.
**Preview:** We're onboarding a limited number of clients into our marketing service this quarter. Here's the deal.

**Purpose:** Urgency + offer. First month free (or heavily discounted) for clients who book before the end of the quarter. Limited spots create genuine scarcity — GRO can only take on a finite number of managed marketing clients per quarter.
**CTA:** Claim your spot → [contact/calendar link]
**Key message:** This is a limited-capacity service. We onboard in batches to ensure quality. This quarter's cohort is filling up.

---

## Design Notes
- All emails use GRO Digital dark-mode brand: `#0d0d0d` background, `#0077c2` blue CTA, white body text
- Logo wordmark text only (no image dependency): **gro digital**
- One CTA button per email — no link clutter
- Mobile-first layout, 600px max-width
- No hero images in emails 2-4 (faster load, cleaner on mobile); Email 1 has a single bold visual

## Metrics to Track
- Open rate: target >35% (warm list)
- Click-through rate: target >4%
- Replies/enquiries from each email
- Calendar bookings attributed to sequence
