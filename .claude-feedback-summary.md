## Infrastructure page — Fly.io monitoring

Added a superAdmin-only Infrastructure page that connects to the Fly.io Machines API and surfaces all apps across multiple orgs with estimated month-to-date spend per app.

**Files touched**: `server/_core/env.ts`, `server/fly-client.ts` (new), `server/fly-pricing.ts` (new), `server/fly-cache.ts` (new), `drizzle/schema.ts`, `drizzle/0050_fly_apps.sql` (new), `server/db.ts`, `server/routers.ts`, `client/src/App.tsx`, `client/src/components/DashboardLayout.tsx`, `client/src/pages/Infrastructure.tsx` (new)

**Why**: One page to see every Fly.io app GD operates, its status, regions, and estimated MTD cost — especially for the Fundi suite — without clicking through the Fly dashboard manually.
